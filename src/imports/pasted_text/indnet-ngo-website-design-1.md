Design a premium, modern, international NGO website for India Network Foundation (INDNET).

The goal is NOT to redesign only the visuals.

The goal is to completely rethink the user experience while preserving the organization's mission and credibility.

The current website looks outdated and does not communicate the impact or trustworthiness of the organization.

Create a website that feels comparable in quality to:

UNICEF
Gates Foundation
Doctors Without Borders
World Vision
Charity Water
International Development Innovation Network (IDIN)

The final design should look like an award-winning NGO website that could win Awwwards while remaining extremely usable and trustworthy.
